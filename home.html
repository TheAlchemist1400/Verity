About us

At Verity, we believe in the power of simplicity. Our brand is built on the idea that less is more — stripping away distractions to focus on what truly matters: form, function, and authenticity. Every piece we create is designed with intention, bridging the gap between timeless style and contemporary culture. We see clothing not just as garments, but as a blank canvas for self-expression, where minimal design meets maximum impact. With a focus on quality, sustainability, and modern aesthetics, verity represents a mindset of clarity in a cluttered world, A place where elegence meets simplicity in Vaal triangle a city driven by clothes. Verity caters for streetwear style and young money elegance style, inspired by the lack of diversity in SA fashion, art industry and the vaal cluture. This brand looks forward to conjuring and rebranding South African fashion industry while cleaning up vaal.

Founders

  • Pharrel Williams

    Pharrell Lanscilo Williams , also known mononymously as Pharrell, is an American musician, singer, songwriter, rapper, record producer, and fashion designer.

  • Samuel Ross

    Samuel Ross is a British fashion designer, creative director, and artist. He is known for founding the fashion label A-COLD-WALL*, Industrial Design studio SR_A SR_A, VERITY and the Black British Artists Grants Programme.

  • Virgil Abloh

    Virgil Abloh was an American fashion designer and entrepreneur. A trained architect, Abloh founded his own line of luxury streetwear clothing under the moniker Pyrex Vision in 2012, which he transformed into the Milan based fashion label Off-White in 2013.
